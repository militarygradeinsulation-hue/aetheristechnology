Automated analysis vs verified audit · Updated October 30, 2026

What can an automated website audit actually prove?

Your website is part of the business. It is not the whole business. Website analysis is useful for spotting visible issues, but cannot validate internal follow-up, staff handoffs, CRM data, financial losses or how the company operates without additional authorized evidence.

How does an automated website report work?

The system examines publicly accessible website information that you provide, analyzes visible signals and produces a report for review. Actual findings depend on the site's accessibility and the scanner's capabilities. The output is a diagnostic starting point, not proof of a financial leak, recovered revenue or a guaranteed result.

What a public website report can help investigate

Customer journey clarity

Is the service understandable? Is there a visible path to ask for a quote or appointment? Do key pages clearly explain the next step?

Visible conversion obstacles

Look for confusing calls to action, incomplete messaging and public contact paths. A report cannot independently know how many customers abandoned the page.

Website information consistency

Review repeated claims, unclear service coverage or contradictory public instructions. A page issue is not necessarily an internal process failure.

Technical and information signals

Where accessible, check structured information and obvious technical barriers. Only tested performance measurements support specific speed claims.

What it cannot verify without your records

Lost sales or profit

Attributing a dollar loss requires qualified inquiry records, outcomes, margin, and a plausible causal link.

Employee performance or psychology

A website cannot establish employees' working styles, personal traits or competency. Do not profile people from website text.

Operational process failures

Sales-to-production handoffs, purchase approvals and CRM discipline require direct internal evidence.

Guaranteed recovery

A recommendation can be tested but is not an assurance of results. Report findings may be incomplete or require human verification.

Frequently asked questions

Is an automatic report the same as a Golden Report?

No. The digital website product evaluates public web information. A Golden Report may involve a larger evidence-based review and approval process; it should not be implied to run instantly for an anonymous visitor.

When will I get a paid digital report?

After confirmed payment and successful processing, the existing Aetheris digital delivery flow makes a report available on the checkout return page. Processing can fail, so the UI must show pending and failed status rather than a fake completion promise.

Can a report show exactly how much profit I am losing?

Not from a public website alone. Your own self-reported figures can illustrate scenarios, but actual lost profit requires verified operational and financial evidence.

Can I scan my competitor's website?

The automated product is intended for publicly accessible business websites. Respect website access rules and submit only a legitimate target. It does not require credentials or private documents.
