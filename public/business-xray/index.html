Answer-first diagnostic / What this actually does

What is a business friction audit?

It traces where a task moves between people, processes and software, then separates observed problems from possible financial impact. Business X-Ray starts with your own inputs, not hidden access to your company. Your free figures are unverified estimates; checking records is a separate service.

Lost inquiries

Count unanswered calls, forms and messages. Check CRM and call records before valuing a potential recovery.

People and handoffs

Find unclear ownership, repeated approvals and missing next steps. Measure the time spent chasing work.

Tools and re-entry

Record duplicate entry and disconnected information. Verify staff hours before calling them savings.
