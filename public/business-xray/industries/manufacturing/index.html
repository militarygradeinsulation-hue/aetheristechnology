Manufacturing Workflow Audit: RFQ Delays, Rework and ERP Handoffs

A quote can stall before it reaches an estimator. Engineering may revise a drawing after purchasing begins. Production can redo work because two systems disagree about the current revision. The diagnosis is about the flow, not the person.

How do machine shops and specialty manufacturers identify hidden operational costs?

Trace a real order from RFQ to quote, engineering revision, job release, machining, inspection and invoicing. Measure turnaround time, rekeying hours and preventable rework using production records. Prioritize observed bottlenecks before purchasing additional software.

Four workflows worth examining

RFQ intake and quote turnaround

Measure elapsed time and touches from inbound RFQ to a complete quote. Separate customer waits from internal review waits.

Evidence: RFQ timestamp, assigned estimator, missing inputs, quote send date

First correction: Assign the next owner and timebox incomplete RFQs

Engineering revision control

Compare revision identifiers in drawings, ERP job travelers, purchasing and inspection. Confirm which is authoritative.

Evidence: Released revision, travelers, nonconformance reports, ECO log

First correction: Add a revision confirmation gate before purchase/job release

Shop-floor and CRM re-entry

Have operators observe one representative job and log the same fields typed in multiple tools.

Evidence: ERP transactions, CRM touches, spreadsheets, minutes per transfer

First correction: Remove one duplicate entry task after verifying the authoritative record

Rework and inspection handoffs

Separate scrap from rework and customer change orders. Look for causes tied to requirements and handoffs, not personality.

Evidence: Scrap reports, hours, NCR records, first-pass yield

First correction: Trend the cause codes weekly and fix a recurring failure

The 30-day validation sequence

  1. Week 1. Choose one workflow, owner and baseline. Save a small representative sample of source records.
  2. Week 2. Trace the handoff and measure what actually happened; distinguish missing data from a confirmed problem.
  3. Week 3. Pilot one no-cost correction on a limited set of new jobs; avoid introducing a subscription before you know the cause.
  4. Week 4. Compare before and after; decide whether the effect is material, repeatable and worth extending.

Questions owners ask

Can a machine shop assess waste without exposing ERP records?

Yes. The free X-Ray accepts estimated counts and hours locally. Any deeper integration or file review should be separately authorized.

Should the first project be a new ERP?

Usually diagnose the workflow first. If revision ownership, handoffs or reporting discipline are the root cause, a new system may reproduce the same problem.

How does this relate to a Golden Report?

Business X-Ray identifies hypotheses to investigate. A separate verified Golden Report may examine actual documents, system evidence and correction options with authorization.

Does the tool evaluate employee psychology?

No. It asks about observable working patterns and process friction. It does not diagnose personnel or assign psychometric scores.
