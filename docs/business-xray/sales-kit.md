# Sales kit: Aetheris Business X-Ray

## Simple positioning

**Your company may not need another tool. It may need to understand where work is breaking down.**

Business X-Ray traces three practical sources of friction: commercial follow-up, employee capacity, and the points where people, processes, and systems stop aligning. The free snapshot shows what to investigate. The paid Verified X-Ray distinguishes assumptions from documented evidence and recommends what to fix first.

## Ideal first buyers

Owner-led B2B services, construction/fabrication, industrial services, and small professional service businesses with multiple handoffs. Start with the founders and operators you already know, not a broad ad campaign.

## Suggested offers to test

- **Free self-assessment**: online, self-service; no contact details needed.
- **$199 Verified X-Ray**: clearly scoped independent document review and an actionable brief. Requires owner-provided workflow details and basic operating numbers; no promise of increased revenue.
- **$599 30-Day Correction Roadmap**: owner-approved plan with priorities, owners, dates, validation measures, and integration considerations. Build only after buyer demand is confirmed.

## First-customer validation

1. Share the free link with five trusted owner-operators.
2. Watch how they use it; ask which question they couldn't answer and whether the output reflects a real issue.
3. Offer a paid verified review to the people who acknowledge a material problem.
4. Document proof from signed-off client inputs, not hypothetical conversion values.
5. Improve only the questions and recommendations that led to real conversations or purchases.

## Sale guardrails

Keep paid services bounded in scope, include a written consent and data handling statement, and make it clear that estimated financial opportunity is not the same as recoverable profit. Avoid psychological inference from employee profiles or public communications unless ethically appropriate and grounded in consent and sufficient evidence.
