## Five Formats

1. **The Case File** (flagship, 2x/week) — CASE ID, STATUS: ACTIVE, THE FINDING, THE EVIDENCE, THE MATH, THE FIX (teased), THE LESSON
2. **Leak of the Week** (1x/week) — Name one leak pattern, define it, show the signs, teach them to spot it, don't give away the fix
3. **The Dead Simple Diagnostic** (1x/week) — One 60-second test, THE TEST, THE THRESHOLD, WHAT IT MEANS
4. **Operator's Journal** (1-2x/week) — 3-8 lines, no template, no CTA, field notes, personal, unpolished
5. **The Contrarian** (1x/week) — THE CLAIM, THE EVIDENCE, THE COUNTER, THE POSITION. Defensible dissent, not rage-bait.

## Seven Rules

1. Every post finds a leak, names a leak, or fixes a leak. Otherwise don't post.
2. No AI tells. No "As an AI-enabled…" No "In the age of AI…"
3. Every number is specific or it doesn't exist. "$1.4M/year" not "millions."
4. Every CTA = "Run the 14-Point Leak Audit." One door. (Operator's Journal has NO CTA.)
5. Never explain methodology unprompted. Demonstrate it.
6. One-Sentence Test: could an AI-consultant LinkedIn bot have written this? If yes, rewrite.
7. When in doubt — cut it.

## Blog / Long-Form Style

- Lead with forensic frame. First 3 lines match Case File DNA.
- H2s as dossier section markers: "THE INVENTORY", "THE AUTOPSY", "THE MATH", "THE FIX", "THE PATTERN"
- Numbers in digits, currency explicit, time frames specific
- Break every 3-4 sentences. Air on the page.
- Every blog ends with single clean CTA: the Leak Audit, no alternatives
- No generic intro paragraphs. Start in the middle.

## Playbook Style

- Treat as field manual, not consulting deck
- Numbered points, mono labels (POINT 1, POINT 2…)
- Each point: Question, Find, Threshold, Why it bleeds
- No fluff. No "about the author." No executive summary that repeats intro.
- Appendix at back if needed. Signed at end in first person. Brief.
- Tone of a post-op briefing. Calm. Factual. Numbered.

## Weekly Rotation

Mon: Case File | Tue: Operator's Journal | Wed: Leak of the Week | Thu: Diagnostic | Fri: Contrarian | Sat: Operator's Journal | Sun: Case File
