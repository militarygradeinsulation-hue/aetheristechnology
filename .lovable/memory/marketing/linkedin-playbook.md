## The Three Gears (run simultaneously, daily)

1. **Borrowed Distribution** — Comment-Jack 5 industry giants daily within 30 min of their post + reciprocity pods of 8–12 non-competing creators.
2. **Forensic Content** — Expensive Mistake Hooks + Audit Carousels (7x dwell vs text). Carousels and cheat sheets get forced distribution.
3. **Surgical Outbound** — Loom audits + Soft Front Door DMs + Reverse-Engineered demand search.

## Post Architecture Rules

**Hook formula (mandatory for Case Files & Leak of the Week):**
`$[specific dollar] + [specific %] + [named wound]`
Example: *"This $4M company was losing 31% of inbound leads because of a single CRM field nobody touched in 2 years."*

**Format mix per week (7 posts):**
- 2 Case Files (Mon + Sun) — 1 must be a 7–10 slide carousel
- 1 Leak of the Week (Wed)
- 1 Diagnostic (Thu) — carousel/cheat-sheet eligible
- 2 Operator's Journal (Tue + Sat) — NO CTA
- 1 Contrarian (Fri)

**Autopsy Series (mandatory):**
Tuesday Case File is always titled `Autopsy #N: [vertical] — [the leak]`. Numbered, serialized, builds permanent searchable IP. Autopsy counter increments weekly.

## The Soft Front Door (every CTA-eligible post)

Every post except Operator's Journal ends with: `Comment [KEYWORD] for the [asset]`.
- Keyword is unique per post (AUTOPSY, BLEED, LEAK14, CHECKLIST, etc.)
- Comment velocity spikes the algorithm
- DM the asset with ZERO pitch
- Follow with ONE curious question about their business
- Never pitch in the DM

## Surgical Outbound Scripts

**Loom Audit (20–40% reply rate vs 1–2% cold InMail):**
> "Found 3 things on your site costing you leads. 3-min video here. Free, no pitch."
3-minute screen recording pointing out 2–3 specific visible leaks. Targets mid-level Marketing/Ops managers, NOT CEOs.

**Reverse-Engineered Demand Search (daily):**
LinkedIn search → "looking for help with [HubSpot / lead flow / CRM / pipeline / attribution]" → Filter: Posts → Sort: Latest. Comment thoughtfully (no pitch). 24h later DM:
> "Saw your post about [X]. I work on exactly this. Happy to do a free 20-min teardown of where the leak likely is. No pitch — just curious if my hypothesis is right."

**Mid-Level Bypass DM:**
> "Noticed your team posted about scaling — I help companies your size find leaks before scaling makes them worse. Happy to share a 1-page framework."

## Daily Choreography (5 timed blocks, ~3 hours total)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 08:00 | Drop 1 Forensic Content piece + engage Reciprocity Pod within 30 min |
| 09:00 | Comment-Jack the top 5 industry giants with sharp, additive insights |
| 11:00 | Record + dispatch 10 Loom audits to mid-level targets |
| 14:00 | Reverse-Engineered demand search → comment on 5–10 posts |
| 16:00 | Reply to post comments + send Soft Front Door DM assets |

## Weekly Rep Volume Targets

- 50 Loom audits
- 100 thoughtful comments
- 20 warm conversations triggered
- 5–15 booked discovery calls

## The 90-Day Activation Curve

- Day 0–30: **The Quit Zone** — flat output, where most consultants fail
- Day 30–60: First inbound trickles
- Day 60+: **Inbound Engine triggers** — compounding flywheel

Rule: never judge results before day 90. The algorithm rewards consistency, not intensity.

## The 95/5 / 7-Month Buying Cycle

95% of the audience isn't buying right now. The buying window opens ~7 months after first useful exposure. Build IP for the 95%, capture the 5% via Soft Front Door.

## What Forbidden

- Posting on the fly / morning dopamine hunting (batch-write only)
- Pitching in the first DM
- Targeting CEOs cold (always mid-level operators)
- Generic motivational quotes (always specific teardowns)
- Quitting before day 90
