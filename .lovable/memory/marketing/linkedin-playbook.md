## The Philosophical Frame: B2P (not B2B)

Every buying decision is a fusion of rational data + emotional resonance. Sterile corporate-speak is dead. Humanity is the moat in an AI-saturated market.

**The 95/5 Rule:** 95% of your market is NOT buying today. Personal brand is the mechanism for top-of-mind recall when the 95% becomes the 5%. Build IP for the 95%, capture the 5% via Soft Front Door.

**The Human Advantage matrix:** AI = volume/speed/recycled data (table stakes). Human = creativity/judgment/lived experience (differentiator).

## The Six Content Pillars (pre-validated, no random topics)

Every post must map to one of:
1. **AI** (operational AI, not hype)
2. **Startups & Scaleups**
3. **Leadership**
4. **Culture**
5. **Personal Brand / Operator IP**
6. **Digital Business / Revenue Systems**

For Aetheris specifically: skew toward 1, 2, 6 with Forensic angle. Use 3, 4, 5 for Operator's Journal.

## The Three Gears (run simultaneously, daily)

1. **Borrowed Distribution** — Comment-Jack 5 industry giants daily within 30 min of their post + reciprocity pods of 8–12 non-competing creators.
2. **Forensic Content** — Expensive Mistake Hooks + Audit Carousels (7x dwell vs text). Carousels and cheat sheets get forced distribution.
3. **Surgical Outbound** — Loom audits + Soft Front Door DMs + Reverse-Engineered demand search.

## Post Architecture Rules

**Hook formula (mandatory for Case Files & Leak of the Week):**
`$[specific dollar] + [specific %] + [named wound]`
Example: *"This $4M company was losing 31% of inbound leads because of a single CRM field nobody touched in 2 years."*

**Format mix per week (7 posts):**
- 2 Case Files (Mon + Sun) — 1 must be a 7–10 slide carousel (Monday = Autopsy)
- 1 Leak of the Week (Wed)
- 1 Diagnostic (Thu) — carousel/cheat-sheet eligible
- 2 Operator's Journal (Tue + Sat) — NO CTA, raw field notes
- 1 Contrarian (Fri)

**Autopsy Series (mandatory):**
Monday Case File is always titled `Autopsy #N: [vertical] — [the leak]`. Numbered, serialized, builds permanent searchable IP. Counter increments weekly.

## The Content Hierarchy (by reach)

1. **Carousels & Cheat Sheets** — 7x reach, "watch time king"
2. **Infographics** — scroll-stop visual hook
3. **Short-form Video** — captioned, personality-driven
4. **Thought Leadership Ads** — promote depth, never product specs

LinkedIn is now the #2 most-cited source for LLMs. Deep framework-heavy content future-proofs authority in AI search.

## The Soft Front Door (every CTA-eligible post)

Every post except Operator's Journal ends with: `Comment [KEYWORD] for the [asset]`.
- Keyword unique per post (AUTOPSY, BLEED, LEAK14, CHECKLIST, etc.)
- Comment velocity spikes the algorithm
- DM the asset with ZERO pitch
- Follow with ONE curious question about their business
- Never pitch in the DM

## Surgical Outbound Scripts

**Loom Audit (20–40% reply rate vs 1–2% cold InMail):**
> "Found 3 things on your site costing you leads. 3-min video here. Free, no pitch."
3-minute screen recording pointing out 2–3 specific visible leaks. Targets mid-level Marketing/Ops, NOT CEOs.

**Reverse-Engineered Demand Search (daily):**
LinkedIn search → "looking for help with [HubSpot / lead flow / CRM / pipeline / attribution]" → Filter: Posts → Sort: Latest. Comment thoughtfully (no pitch). 24h later DM:
> "Saw your post about [X]. I work on exactly this. Happy to do a free 20-min teardown of where the leak likely is. No pitch — just curious if my hypothesis is right."

**Mid-Level Bypass DM:**
> "Noticed your team posted about scaling — I help companies your size find leaks before scaling makes them worse. Happy to share a 1-page framework."

## The Golden Metrics (only three numbers that matter)

1. **Daily Follower Growth** — definitive brand health check
2. **Repost Ratio** — target 20% repost-to-like (proves "claimable" value)
3. **Newsletter Subscriber Growth** — the "deplatforming" metric (rented → owned)

Ignore vanity (likes, impressions in isolation).

## Weekly Rep Volume Targets

- 50 Loom audits
- 100 thoughtful comments
- 20 warm conversations triggered
- 5–15 booked discovery calls

## The 3-Step Batching Process (never post live)

1. **Ideation Only** — 20+ ideas in one headphones-in session
2. **Writing & Design Briefs** — handed off, structured into posts + visual briefs
3. **Approvals** — final voice + Song Sheet alignment check

Delegate everything except the Magic (final approval + unique POV).

## The 90-Day Execution Plan

**Days 1–30 (Quit Zone — most quit here):**
- Define 6 pillars + Song Sheet
- 100 thoughtful comments/week on giant accounts
- Establish daily choreography rhythm

**Days 31–60 (Flatline — first inbound trickles):**
- Launch Business Autopsy series
- 50 Loom audits/week to targeted prospects
- Carousel cadence stable

**Days 61–90 (Ignition → Inbound Engine):**
- 3-step batching system in place
- VA for DM management
- Newsletter launched to capture deplatformed traffic

## The 95/5 / 7-Month Buying Cycle

95% of audience isn't buying right now. Buying window opens ~7 months after first useful exposure. Build IP for the 95%, capture the 5% via Soft Front Door.

## "Talia's Challenge" Reframe

For career-transition language: never "Open to Work" (seeker stigma). Always "Open to [outcome]" — e.g., "Open to Drive EBITDA," "Open to Triple Your Sales." Value provider posture, not job seeker.

## Forbidden

- Posting on the fly / morning dopamine hunting (batch-write only)
- Pitching in the first DM
- Targeting CEOs cold (always mid-level operators)
- Generic motivational quotes (always specific teardowns)
- Quitting before day 90
- Vanity metric obsession (likes/impressions in isolation)
- Random topics outside the Six Pillars
