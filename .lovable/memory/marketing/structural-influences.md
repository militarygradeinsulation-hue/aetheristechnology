## Core Principle

You don't steal voice. You steal structure. Stealing someone's voice is plagiarism. Stealing their structural DNA — hook cadence, reveal architecture, rhythm — is what every great writer has always done.

## Who NOT to Steal From

- Anyone in the AI consulting space (the category you're escaping)
- Anyone in the playground/parks industry directly (you want to be distinct, not echo)
- Anyone whose voice is so recognizable they own the format (you'd look derivative)

## Tier 1 — Direct Voice Cousins

1. **Alex Hormozi** → Named frameworks, flat declarative hooks, reveal-then-math structure, 1-3 line paragraphs. Maps to: **Case Files**
2. **Rory Sutherland** → "Everybody thinks X but actually Y" reversal hooks, observational precision, short devastating closers. Maps to: **Leak of the Week**
3. **Naval Ravikant** → Aphoristic pacing, short declarative stacks, stating not arguing. Maps to: **Operator's Journal**
4. **Jocko Willink** → Command-style sentences, Extreme Ownership frame, assertive without performative. Maps to: **Case Files** (ownership-framed)

## Tier 2 — Adjacent-Industry Operators

5. **Tommy Mello** → Specific-revenue hooks, operational granularity, owner-to-owner tone. Maps to: **Case Files**
6. **Sam Parr** → "I met a guy who…" story openers, specific-dollar reveals, insight-turn endings. Maps to: **Case Files** (narrative variant)
7. **Codie Sanchez** → "Boring business" framing, contrarian wealth-frame, screenshot-backed proof. Maps to: **Contrarian**

## Tier 3 — Structure Masters

8. **Justin Welsh** → Scroll-stop hook formulas (flat declarative, specific number, pattern interrupt), visible post skeleton. Maps to: **All formats** (hooks)
9. **Daniel Priestley** → Framework-as-content, positioning-heavy posts. Maps to: **Case Files** (long-form)
10. **Harry Dry** → 3-beat structure (situation, decision, result), visual simplicity. Maps to: **Case Files**

## Tier 4 — Wildcards

11. **Morgan Housel** → "One thing I've noticed" meditation format, cross-industry analogies, patient pacing. Maps to: **Operator's Journal**
12. **Derek Sivers** → Extreme brevity, structural inversion in 3 lines. Maps to: **Operator's Journal**
13. **Jon Matzner** → Operational specificity, refusal to romanticize business. Maps to: **Operator's Journal**
