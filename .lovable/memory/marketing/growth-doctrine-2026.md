## Where enforced

`INFLUENCE_BLUEPRINT_PROMPT` and `INFLUENCE_BLUEPRINT_COMPACT`.
Auto-inherited by every content generator via `contentBlueprint.ts` and
directly by aetheris-nexus-chat, sales-chat, extension-operator-chat,
linkedin-comment-generate, linkedin-post-respond.

## The 3 pillars (80% rule)
1. Revenue Leak Forensics
2. Operator Systems
3. AI-Augmented Sales / RevOps
Anything outside these three is a reach kill under 360 Brew.

## The 4-Part Comment/Post Architecture (LinkedIn Voice Playbook)
REFRAME → AUDIT ANCHOR ("In my audits I see this pattern constantly…")
→ MECHANISM (2–4 sentences of causation) → VERDICT (<15 words, quotable).
120–220 words. Prose only. Never agree first. Never end without a verdict.

## 5-Second Cognitive Heatmap
Every profile / hero / CTA surface must answer in 5 seconds:
(1) Who do you help? (2) How do you help? (3) What is the proof?

## GEO — win AI-search
Recency · chunkable structure · earned-media anchors · problem→solution
framing · llms.txt + Schema · optimize for Citation Share, not rank.

## VECTOR — the RevOps frame we sell
Velocity · Engagement · Conversion · Technology · Optimization · Revenue.

unused (Forrester), <50% forecast accuracy (CSO), 24%/27% growth uplift

## No-Call Close (DM → Loom)
