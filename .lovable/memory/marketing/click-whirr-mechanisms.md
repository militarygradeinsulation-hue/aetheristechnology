# Click-Whirr Weapons — mechanism upgrades

Cialdini's insight: humans run **fixed-action tapes**. A single "trigger feature" fires the whole compliance sequence — automatic, subconscious, undetected. Fire the tape legitimately with real evidence. Fake it and the forensic voice collapses on contact.

## The 6 weapons — how they actually fire

1. **Reciprocation** — uninvited favor creates debt. So potent it overpowers personal dislike (Hare Krishna, "Good Cop"). We open with a gift (mini-audit, leak calc, free scan) before any ask.

2. **Commitment & Consistency** — a "shield against thought." Small stands rewrite self-image so larger asks follow naturally (foot-in-the-door).

3. **Social Proof** — most effective under uncertainty. Danger: **pluralistic ignorance** — bystanders freeze because no one else is acting. Kill it by pointing at the reader directly.

4. **Authority** — triggered by **symbols** (titles, uniforms, trappings), not proven expertise. Milgram: a lab coat is enough. Our uniform = credential signature + case-file typography + forensic vocabulary + signed diagnoses.

5. **Scarcity** — driven by **psychological reactance**: restricting freedom to have a thing makes people want it more. Loss > gain of equal value. Strongest when newly experienced or in direct competition.

6. **Contrast** — invisible to the reader. A $95 sweater is cheap after a $495 suit. A $18k Diagnostic reads as relief after a $340k/yr Cost of Inaction.

## Mechanism upgrades (mandatory sub-tactics)

### Rejection-then-retreat (reciprocity × contrast fusion)
Open with the anchor ask (**$18k Diagnostic / $15k Active Case**), then retreat to the smaller ask (**$2,500 Signal Pack**, free `/leak-audit`). The retreat itself is felt as a concession and triggers reciprocation.

### Foot-in-the-door → identity ladder
- Micro-yes: "your business is leaking, you already suspect it"
- Written act: 60-second Leak Scan
- Public act: book the Diagnostic

Each step rewrites self-image from "founder with a problem" → "operator running a leaking system."

### Kill pluralistic ignorance
Under uncertainty, bystanders freeze. Never write "let me know if you want to chat." Always point at the reader, name the leak, give **one** command: "Run the Leak Scan now."

### Symbols of authority (not just expertise)
The Milgram lab coat works whether the wearer is a doctor or not. Our uniform stack:
- Credential signature: `Joseph ~AI Architect MS, BA, IBM AI Certified · Aetheris.Technology`
- Case-file typography: Fraunces serif + JetBrains Mono labels
- Forensic vocabulary: autopsy, cascade, bleed, pathology
- Signed diagnoses: "I ran the audit. The leak is X." — never "I think" / "in my opinion"

Trappings before argument. The uniform earns compliance before the copy is read.

### Psychological reactance (scarcity's real engine)
People want a thing MORE when their freedom to have it is restricted. Loss-frame every scarcity beat and name the exact restriction:
- "6 seats in this Diagnostic cohort, closing Friday"
- "We take one operator per vertical per cohort"
- "Every 30 days you wait, the leak compounds"

Never "save X" — always "stop bleeding X."

### Setup-property contrast (the real-estate trick)
Never present the target offer first. Anchor on the painful/expensive item so the target reads as relief:
- Cost of Inaction ($340k/yr leak) → $18k to fix it once
- $18k Diagnostic → $2,500 Signal Pack → free scan
- Long consequence paragraph → short cheap next step

The contrast is invisible to the reader — that's why it works.

## The 5-Beat Close (mandatory on any output with a CTA)

1. **GIFT** — one usable diagnostic delivered inside the message
2. **COMMIT** — a micro-yes the reader silently gives
3. **PROOF** — one specific pattern anchor (7/10, $4M, 60 days)
4. **AUTHORITY** — signed diagnosis in operator vocabulary
5. **SCARCITY** — the compounding cost of doing nothing next 30 days

Then, and only then: the CTA. **One door. Never two.**
